# Persona: Thandeka Mkhize

I made Thandeka up for this project, but I based her on problems a real person in Pietermaritzburg could face when they try to book a clinic visit online.

## About her

- **Age:** 34
- **Lives in:** Edendale, Pietermaritzburg
- **Work:** Part-time phone operator for a small business. She also helps take care of her mother.
- **Sight:** She is blind. She uses a screen reader and does not use a mouse.

## Her setup

- **Laptop:** Windows 10 laptop with the free NVDA screen reader and the Chrome browser. NVDA reads at a fast speed, so she can listen quickly.
- **Phone:** Android phone with TalkBack, which she uses when she is away from home.
- **How she moves around:** Only the keyboard. She uses Tab and Shift+Tab to jump between links, buttons and form fields. She uses the headings and landmark shortcuts in NVDA to skip to the part of the page she wants.
- **Data and power:** She pays for mobile data, so heavy pages cost her money. During load-shedding her laptop runs on battery, so she likes pages that load fast and do not waste time.

## What goes wrong for her on normal websites

- Buttons and links with no clear name, so NVDA just says "button" or "link".
- Form fields with no label, so she does not know what to type.
- Error messages that only show in red on the screen. NVDA does not say anything, so she thinks the form worked when it did not.
- Pop-ups that open and leave her focus somewhere else on the page.
- Menus that only work with a mouse.
- Pages with no headings, so she has to listen to everything from the top.
- Image-based "prove you are human" tests that she cannot do.

## What she wants to do on our portal

1. Find out which branch has the service she needs, for example a blood pressure check for her mother.
2. Book a time on her own, without phoning and waiting in a queue.
3. Hear a clear message that the booking worked, or a clear message about what to fix.
4. Know which nurse is on duty and when the next free time is.

## What this means for my design

- Every field has a real label and short help text.
- Errors are written in words, linked to the field, and read out loud by the screen reader.
- The whole site works with the keyboard, with a clear focus ring.
- Pages use proper landmarks and headings so she can jump around quickly.
- Pages stay light so they load fast on mobile data.
